{# app/templates/dashboard/cards/clashes.html ───────────────────────────────────────── Clashing Projects card. Collapsed: clash count, or "No clashes" when there are none — unchanged from the stub. At zero clashes the card renders MUTED (see dash_card's muted=True in _dashboard_macros.html): no chevron, not clickable, body permanently collapsed. That's handled entirely by the macro + dashboard.js's click handler (which already skips toggling any .dash-card--muted card) — nothing in this file needs to special-case zero clashes itself. Expanded: "By Deliverable" / "By Project" toggle — the two ways a designer can be double-booked (see compute_clashes() in dashboard_logic.py): - By Deliverable: same designer assigned to 2+ DELIVERABLES (possibly across different projects) due the same day. - By Project: same designer assigned to 2+ WHOLE PROJECTS sharing the same Final Deadline (execution_date). Unlike the Due/Next Actions/Decisions cards, this toggle does NOT fetch anything — `clashes` (from _compute_clashes_response(user), already a context var on every render) contains BOTH breakdowns in full up front, since clash lists are always small (a handful of entries at most, not something worth paginating or filtering server-side). Both panels are server-rendered here; dashboard.js just shows/hides between them, the same "toggle a stored .active/.hidden pair" mechanic already used for the deep-dive zone's Projects/Deliverables tabs (switchTab()). Each clash is a GROUP: one designer, one date, and the 2+ deliverables (or projects) that collide on it — grouped visually under a small "designer — date" heading rather than flattened into individual rows, so it's clear at a glance WHY these items are listed together. Severity (added 10 Jul 2026, By Deliverable only — see _clash_severity() in dashboard_logic.py): each by-deliverable group carries a 'clash'/'potential' severity, shown as a "Clash Detected" (red) or "Potential Clash" (amber) tag next to the designer's name. 'clash' means either the group spans more than one project, or every deliverable in it shares the exact same delivery TIME on the same project — a certain conflict either way. 'potential' means same project, same day, but different (or unset) times — might be fine, worth a second look, not a sure thing. That's also why each deliverable row now shows its own time (or "No time set") — without it there'd be no way to see WHY a group only rated "potential".
