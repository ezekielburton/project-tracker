Cost / Charge / Profit per project are LIFETIME totals — identical to that project's Cost Breakdown, unchanged by which period you view. Only Dev Time (this period's dev-time entries) and which projects appear change with the period. The three cards are the period-scoped figures: Total cost = ledger entries dated in this window; Closed profit = lifetime profit of every project that closed in this period (a closed project overlaps only the one period it closed in — see lib/periods.py); Projected profit = charge minus costs so far across still-open projects touching this period. Closed profit reads in --pine (green), Projected profit in di-column-header--sky (blue) — a deliberate split so "banked" and "still estimated" money don't look interchangeable. Currency figures are whole-number, thousands-separated ('{:,.0f}'); profit also gets an explicit +/- sign ('{:+,.0f}'). Cost Breakdown keeps its 2-decimal ledger style; these are rollups/estimates, so whole numbers read better. Expanded features render as their own rows under the same six columns as the parent (name indented under Project, a stage pill under Status, hours under Dev Time, Cost/Charge/Profit blank). #}
