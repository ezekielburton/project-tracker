# Your dashboard

### 5. `dashboard.overview` — Reading your dashboard

_Status: rewritten for the dashboard menu. Each dashboard's own pages get their text as they are built._

[Paragraph] Your dashboard shows what needs you today. The menu on its left depends on your department and seniority.

[Heading] The menu

[List]
Dashboard opens your first page. For most people that is Overview. The Heads of Design and Client Servicing open on their department page.
A number on a menu entry counts the things on that page that need you. No number means nothing does.
My hub (leave, sick days, reimbursements) shows Soon until the HR update.
Pages not built yet say Coming soon.

[Heading] New briefs

[List]
Your first page lists new briefs: projects with no deliverables yet, newest first, with the client, CS lead and age.
Click one to open the project.
With none, it is one short line.

[Heading] Who sees which pages

[List]
Designers: Overview, My projects, Site visits, Calendar.
Design leads: Overview, Assignments, Team, Calendar. The Head of Design also gets Design workload.
Client Servicing: Overview, Approvals, My clients, Calendar. The Head of Client Servicing also gets Needs attention.
Project Owners: Overview, Approvals, My projects, Site visits, Calendar.
Management: Overview, Escalations, Needs attention, Design workload, Delivery, Teams, Clients, Adoption.
Finance, HR, Production, Logistics and Digital Innovation: Overview.

[Callout] Menu looks wrong? It comes from your department and seniority. Ask an admin to check them in Admin → Accounts.

### 6a. `dashboard.calendar` — The dashboard calendar

_Status: new. Entries arrive with the site visits update._

[Paragraph] One calendar for your work: deadlines, installs, removals, overdue work and site visits.

[List]
The arrows change month; Today brings you back.
Click a day to see its list on the right.
The legend shows what each colour means.
On a phone, each day shows how many entries it has. Tap it for the list.

[Callout] The calendar stays empty until the next update adds entries to it.
