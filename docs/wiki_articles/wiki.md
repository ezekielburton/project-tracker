# Using the wiki

### `wiki.editor` — Writing a wiki article

_Status: written and pasted 5 Oct 2026 (2.6.2)_

```
Section: Using the wiki

[Paragraph]
The Wiki Editor is where articles are written, ordered and kept current. Edit on the wiki home opens it.

[Heading] Writing an article

[List]
+ Article - pick a section, a title, a help key if it explains a page, and a start: How-to, Reference or Blank
Write in blocks: paragraph, heading, list, image, callout, video
Your work autosaves as a draft. Readers see the live version until you save
Save Article makes it live. Tick Published for readers to see it
Saving also counts as a review

[Heading] Video

[List]
Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, or upload an mp4 or webm up to 200MB
The length fills in from an uploaded file. For a link, type it in seconds
Readers always see the video first, wherever it sits in the article

[Heading] Keeping it current

[List]
Review due - not reviewed this quarter. Check it against the app, then press Reviewed, or edit and save
Write next - searches that found nothing, most asked first, with what people were after. Write starts an article with that title. Dismiss drops it
Not useful - articles readers answered No to since the last save, with their notes
Coverage - pages with a ? that have no article yet

[Heading] Sections and order

[List]
Drag sections and articles to reorder. It saves on drop
Relevant roles decide who sees a section first. Empty means everyone. Nothing is ever hidden

[Heading] Unused uploads

[Paragraph]
Images and videos no article uses any more are counted with their size. Clean up deletes them and their NAS backups. Anything uploaded in the last day is kept, in case it sits in an unsaved draft.

[Callout]
Your own reads, searches and votes are never counted, even while previewing as someone else. The numbers are the team's.
```
