# Getting started

### 1. `core.overview` — What OVP is for

_Status: written (earlier chat)_

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
OVP stands for One Vitamin Platform. It is where every job at Vitamin is briefed, scheduled, worked on and signed off. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use OVP.

[Heading] Who it is for

[Paragraph]
Everyone. Client Servicing, designers, team leads, management, production, logistics, HR, finance and HSE all work in the same system. What you see depends on your role, so you are not looking at screens you will never use. There is more on that in Who does what.

[Heading] The one rule

[Paragraph]
All work must be submitted in OVP before it can be worked on. A job that lives only in a WhatsApp message or an email is a job nobody can schedule, track or find six months later. Everything else in this wiki is detail. That part is the rule.

[Heading] What happens where

[List]

Dashboard - what needs you today. This is your landing page when you log in.
Projects - every job in the company, from the brief through to close. The Details, Deliverables and Submissions tabs are where the actual work moves.
Client Servicing - the CS side of the same projects: scheduling, the calendar, invoicing and closed jobs.
Digital Innovation - the DI board, its templates and its archive.
HSE & Compliance - registers, inspections and the HSE calendar.
Directories - the client directory and the file template library.
Wiki - this. Every "how do I..." answer lives here.
App Update Blog - what changed in the app, and when.

[Callout]
Some sidebar items are greyed out. Color Directory and Production Directory are not built yet, and Client Servicing and HSE dim for roles that do not use them. A greyed item is not a broken link.

[Heading] What you do not have to do

[Paragraph]
You do not need to create project folders, name them or move them. The folder structure is generated the moment a brief is submitted, and it follows the same shape every time. You do not need to chase people for a status either - the project's own status is the status, and it updates as work moves.

[Heading] Where to start

[Paragraph]
Read Finding your way around next, then Who does what. After that, go to the article for whichever screen you spend your day in. Every page in the app has a small "?" in its header that opens the article for that page, so you rarely need to come looking for the wiki.
```

### 36. `signal.report` — Reporting a bug or an idea

_Status: draft 2026-10-01, awaiting approval_

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
Signal is the tray on the right edge for telling us what is broken or what is missing. It has two boards: Bug Report and Feature Request.

[Heading] Sending one in

[List]

Open Signal and pick the board
Press Report a bug or New request
Add a title and a description. A bug can also take a severity: High, Med or Low
Submit. Admins are told straight away

[Heading] Reading the boards

[List]

The chips filter by status, each with its count
Bug Report - newest first. In queue, Fix in progress, Testing, Resolved
Feature Request - most upvoted first. Requested, In progress, Testing, Implemented
Implemented ideas drop out of All. Pick the Implemented chip to see them
Each idea shows where Digital Innovation has it: queued, picked up or declined

[Heading] Joining in

[List]

Click the arrow beside an idea to upvote it. Click again to take the vote back
Open any item to comment, or reply to a comment
You can delete your own bug or idea. Only admins change a status or remove comments

[Paragraph]
You are notified when your bug reaches Fix in progress or Resolved, and when your idea reaches In progress or Implemented. The number on the Signal launcher counts new bugs, ideas and Friction Log posts since you last opened it.

[Callout]
Check the board before you post. If it is already there, upvote it or comment instead. One idea with ten votes rises to the top. Ten copies with one vote each do not.
```

### 37. `signal.friction` — The weekly friction log

_Status: draft 2026-10-01, awaiting approval_

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
The Friction Log is the team's weekly list of what is slowing people down in OVP. It is the third tab in the Signal tray and is reviewed every Friday.

[Heading] Who can post

[List]

This week's OVP champions - one per department: Client Servicing, Design, Production, Logistics, Finance
Management and admins
Everyone else can read it

[Paragraph]
Admins set the champions each week in the admin panel. A champion who is not replaced keeps the badge for up to two more weeks, so a department is never left without one.

[Heading] Posting

[List]

Open Signal, then Friction Log
Write what is not working in the box at the bottom
Post. Posts cannot be edited or deleted, so read it over first

[Heading] Reading it

[List]

Grouped by week, newest week first
The current week is tagged Review Fri
Each post shows who wrote it and which department they are champion for

[Callout]
Write the friction, not the fix. "Finding last month's invoices takes five clicks" is more useful than "add an invoices button". The Friday review decides what changes.
```

### 38. `wiki.search` — Searching the wiki

_Status: written and pasted 5 Oct 2026 (2.6.2)_

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
Search reads every word of every article, not just the titles. Type what you are trying to do.

[Heading] Searching

[List]
Use the box on the wiki home, or Search in the rail
Write it the way you would say it: "approve a submission", "log time"
Other forms of a word match too. Approving finds approve
Put words in quotes to match them as a phrase
Put - before a word to leave it out

[Heading] Reading the results

[List]
Title matches rank above body matches
The words you searched are highlighted in each snippet
With your role selected, other roles' articles sit at the bottom, dimmed, tagged with who they are for
Choose Everything under Search to list them all in plain order

[Heading] When nothing is found

[Paragraph]
Your search is recorded so we know what to write next. Add a line on what you were trying to do. It goes straight to the people who write the wiki. If others asked the same, you will see how many.

[Callout]
Nothing found means the article does not exist yet, not that you searched wrong. Your search has been noted. Ask in chat for now.
```

### 39. `wiki.reading` — Reading the wiki

_Status: written and pasted 5 Oct 2026 (2.6.2)_

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
The wiki opens on your role. Everything is still there. Nothing is hidden.

[Heading] Finding your way

[List]
Home - search, Start here for your role, Recently updated and Most read this month
Start here - the basics, then your role's first articles, with each video's length
The rail lists every section. Sections for other roles start folded. Click one to open it
Your role or Everything, under Search, switches between your view and the whole wiki

[Heading] An article

[List]
The video comes first. The written steps below it are the reference
The line under the title says when it was updated, by whom, and when it was last reviewed
On this page jumps to each heading
Related lists others from the same section

[Heading] Useful?

[Paragraph]
At the end of every article. Answer Yes or No, and change it any time. No asks what was missing, and that goes straight to the people who write the wiki.

[Heading] From anywhere in the app

[Paragraph]
The ? on a page opens its article in the Help tray. Open full article brings you here.

[Callout]
Reviewed this quarter means someone checked the article against the app recently. If an article does not match what you see, press No and say what is different.
```

### 40. Light and dark mode (no help key)

_Status: written 5 Oct 2026; colours updated for 2.6.3 (brand tangerine in both themes). No page has a "?" for it, so it has no help key._

```
Section: Getting started

[Paragraph]
OVP has a light and a dark theme. Use whichever is easier on your eyes.

[Heading] Switching

[List]
The toggle in the header
Or Settings, then Appearance, then Dark Mode

[Heading] Where it is saved

[List]
Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to any device
The last change wins. Switch on one device and the other follows next time it loads

[Heading] Colours

[List]
Text is tuned to be readable in both themes
Orange is our brand tangerine, in both themes

[Callout]
If something is hard to read in one theme, report it in Signal with a screenshot and say which theme you were in.
```
